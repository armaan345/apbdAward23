2nd Year ABPD Showbiz Awards 2026

Asli Baharan Punjab Diyan (ABPD), established in 1994 in the United Kingdom, is the UK’s first mixed Bhangra, Gidha, and Modern Dance group.

With over 32 years of excellence, ABPD continues to promote Punjabi culture globally through international performances, collaborations, and cultural showcases.

The ABPD Showbiz Awards 2026, proudly presented by 4 Pillars Entertainments, celebrates outstanding achievements in entertainment, media, business, and community excellence.
