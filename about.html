About Us

Asli Baharan Punjab Diyan (ABPD)

Established in 1994 in the United Kingdom, ABPD is the UK’s first mixed Bhangra, Gidha, and Modern Dance group. For over 32 years, we have been promoting Punjabi culture across global platforms.

From international concerts to film collaborations, ABPD has showcased Punjabi heritage worldwide and worked alongside top artists and productions.

2nd Year ABPD SHOWBIZ AWARDS 2026

"THE CENTRE BANQUETING SUITE 20 MERRICK ROAD, SOUTHALL, UB2 4AU | 9th May 2026 | London"

In celebration of its enduring legacy, ABPD proudly introduced the ABPD Showbiz Awards on 26 April 2025 — the inaugural edition set a gold standard. Now, 4 Pillars Entertainments proudly presents the 2nd Year ABPD Showbiz Awards 2026, taking place on Saturday, 9 May 2026, commemorating the organisation’s 32nd Anniversary alongside a new era of recognition.

This edition brings bigger celebrations, international collaborations, and a salute to the vibrant cultural legacy of the Punjabi & British Asian community.

Recognition & Honours

This prestigious awards ceremony recognises the contributions of the British Indian diaspora across:

  • Cultural Landscape
  • Language & Arts
  • Business & Entrepreneurship
  • Community Initiatives
  • Culinary Excellence

Presented by 4 Pillars Entertainments: elevating the 2nd year with unmatched grandeur and global spotlight.

Leadership & Vision

Under the visionary leadership of Manjit Singh Bhambra, ABPD continues to inspire and promote Punjabi culture across the globe. Partnering with 4 Pillars Entertainments for the 2nd Year ABPD Showbiz Awards 2026 represents a milestone of excellence, innovation and entertainment.
